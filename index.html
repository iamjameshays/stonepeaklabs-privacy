Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 30, 2026

Spectra - Audio Analyzer ("the App") is developed by Stone Peak Labs. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Summary

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Microphone Access

The App requires microphone access to perform real-time audio frequency analysis. All audio processing happens entirely on your device. Audio data is:

  • Analyzed in real-time for frequency visualization only
  • Never recorded or saved to storage
  • Never transmitted to any server
  • Never shared with any third party

Data Collection

The App does not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Audio recordings

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Internet Access

The App does not require internet access and functions entirely offline.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App collects no information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: privacy@stonepeaklabs.com
