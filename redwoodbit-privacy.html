Some of our apps may request device permissions (such as microphone, camera, or storage access) to provide core functionality. When permissions are used:

Our apps do not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services unless explicitly stated in the app.

Most of our apps function entirely offline and do not require internet access unless explicitly stated.

Our apps do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our apps collect no information from any user.
